Before you enter

Questions

Do I need a racing licence?

Class A requires a national or FIM licence.

What happens if I cannot finish a day?

You keep the points you have scored.

Is there fuel and service on the course?

Two refuel zones, at roughly km 34 and km 71.

Where do we sleep?

The paddock is open from the Tuesday before the race.

Can spectators watch?

The prologue on 13 May is free and built for spectators.
